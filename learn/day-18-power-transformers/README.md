Today, I am gonna learn about Power transformers and it has its types
- `Box cox transformers:` The exponent here is a variable called lambda (1) that
varies over the range of -5 to 5, and in the process of
searching, we examine all values of 1. Finally, we choose
the optimal value (resulting in the best approximation to a
normal distribution) for your variable.

- `yeo-johnson transformers:` This transformation is somewhat of an adjustment to the
Box-Cox transformation, by which we can apply it to
negative numbers.
